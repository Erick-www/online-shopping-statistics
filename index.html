D Addition Rule

The addition rule calculates the probability that at least one of two events occurs.

P(E ∪ C) = P(E) + P(C) − P(E ∩ C)

0% Orders in Both Categories are inside P(E) and also inside P(C), so adding the two would count them twice. We subtract P(E ∩ C) once to fix this.
